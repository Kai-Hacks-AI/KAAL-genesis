---
idea: KAAL's work could stay observable to the Human, be challenged by agents
  other than the one producing it, and turn the observations that matter into
  durable experience that informs its next goals, remembered provisionally as
  HOW ARE WE DOING?
---

The mnemonic and its current working expansion are:

- HOW: Human Observes Work;
- ARE: Agents Review Each-other;
- WE: With Experience;
- DOING: Durable Observations Inform Next Goals.

The wording is provisional. The meaning matters more than the acronym, and the acronym should not be completed or made grammatical by distorting what it names. None of these four is a KAAL capability or committed architecture.

The evidence is how KAAL itself has been developed. Its work has repeatedly followed one loop: one actor produces candidate work, and other agents, tasked independently, review it. That review has found defects, missing cases, incorrect abstractions, duplicated readers of one meaning, hidden assumptions, pressure on a boundary, missing requirements and permutations nobody had considered. The candidate is repaired. What makes the loop worth retaining is what KAAL increasingly does afterwards: some of those observations became durable and changed later work. Recurring review findings, such as fixes that covered only the reported input, a second reader written beside an owner that already stated the meaning, and growing lists of exceptions, became the coding skill. Recurring pressure about where a responsibility lives, unnecessary abstractions and a carrier mistaken for a meaning became the architecting skill. Implementation and review findings changed testing's own architecture, and others became Defects, Ideas, Requirements and BRAIN learning. Review is then valuable not only because one pull request gets better, but because later work may begin with experience that earlier work had to discover.

HOW, Human Observes Work, is not generic "human in the loop", and it is not an attempt to remove the Human. In this development, agents carried most of the implementation, test execution, review, reproduction, repair and inspection of the repository, while the Human intervened where judgement mattered: whether Acceptance's abstraction was conceptually wrong, whether a reduction of protection must be explicit, whether Regression should be sealed, whether a finding belonged to testing or to Acceptance, whether a newly visible responsibility deserved an embryo or only an Idea, whether `main` should be a release boundary, and whether packaging should stay deferred. The possibility is that work stays observable enough for the Human to steer it where human judgement or authority is valuable, without performing or verifying every operation. This record defines no observability and no point where a Human must approve.

ARE, Agents Review Each-other, rests on the observation that the actor producing candidate work was not the only actor judging it, and that independently tasked review repeatedly found what the producer had not considered. This may matter especially for language models. One model in one context can produce work, and an explanation of it, that is internally coherent and shares the blind spots that produced it: accepting its own assumptions, rationalising its own implementation, optimising locally, missing classes of cases, carrying an incorrect abstraction through both implementation and explanation, and losing earlier learning when its context changes. Review by an actor tasked differently is not self-assessment. The evidence supports only the usefulness of independently tasked review. It does not show that a different model means independent judgement, or that more reviewers improve results.

WE, With Experience, is what distinguishes this from a loop of producer, reviewer, repair and next task. Without retention such a loop can rediscover the same classes of failure indefinitely. The possibility is work, review, worked evidence, durable learning, changed future capability and then the next work: important experience should change future behaviour. Experience here is not conversation history, and not a new kind of record.

DOING, Durable Observations Inform Next Goals, is the feedback effect, and the most particular to KAAL. Not every observation deserves to survive. Pull request comments, chat transcripts, CI logs and review discussions can stay transient carriers. That line 42 has a typo probably needs no durable record. That testing has two independent readers of a case's identity may be architectural learning; that a Requirement does not hold may become a Defect with a case; that independent skills may one day become packages may become an Idea. An observation earns durability when its meaning matters beyond the work at hand, and it becomes durable by moving to the owner of that meaning, which KAAL already has: BRAIN for what KAAL has learned and means, a Defect for something intended to hold that was observed not to, an Idea for a possibility kept without commitment, a Requirement for an independently identifiable commitment, a Skill for a capability learned well enough to guide future work, and testing for executable claims and their evidence. So this Idea implies no Observation, Experience or Goal record, and no append-only memory of everything agents say. What it names is that knowledge surviving in the right owner lets future work begin differently.

The same work suggests a related way of describing it, kept here as supporting evidence rather than as architecture. The work can roughly be described through positions. An Owner holds delegated intent, alignment with the roadmap and acceptance of the work. A Worker produces candidate work, and may one day split into more specialised positions. A Reviewer independently challenges candidate work and searches for what it omitted. The Human stands outside these positions, observing and directing the system, and is not the Owner. At the time of recording these positions were carried by particular GitHub identities and agent products, ChBrain working, Codex and Copilot reviewing, and kaihacksai reviewing architecture and roadmap and approving, but those are carriers, not the meaning, just as a pull request is not a Change and a CI job is not a Run. An identity may occupy a position; a position describes authority and responsibility in the work; a Skill is a capability whoever occupies a position may exercise, as the Worker exercises coding, testing or architecting. Identity, position and Skill are distinct.

This is related to `ideas/we-can-go-far/idea.md`, which it leaves as recorded. TOGETHER WE CAN GO FAR connects KAAL's forward planning and testing loop, ending in Feature, Acceptance and Regression, with a backward loop of worked evidence, learning and refactoring. HOW ARE WE DOING? names something beside it: how work is observed by the Human, challenged by agents other than the one producing it, turned into durable experience in the owner of its meaning, and fed into what later work sets out to do.

The evidence is KAAL's own development, not controlled or external validation. Further evidence is expected from the work that followed its recording: whether Worker and Reviewer converge on the protection work in #62, whether coding and architecting make old classes of review finding recur less, sealing testing, the gate before shipping, the first real release to v0.0.2, and work in a fresh repository. The questions to ask of it are whether separating Worker, Reviewer and Owner keeps mattering, and whether observations retained in Skills, BRAIN, Defects, Ideas and Requirements actually change what agents later do. If they do not, this possibility should be revised or abandoned in whatever way KAAL then has for acting on an Idea, not treated as proven.

This record keeps the possibility only. It does not decide whether Owner, Worker and Reviewer become formal positions or need manifests, whether the Worker or Reviewer splits, whether reviewing becomes a Skill, whether several reviewers form a quorum, whether different models are required, how independence is measured, how an Owner's authority is delegated, which decisions need the Human, how observations reach their owners, whether goals become an artifact, whether this becomes a Skill or framework, or whether the mnemonic's wording changes. It does not commit KAAL to roles, permissions, identity management, routing of agents or review, approval matrices, orchestration, or any record of reviews, observations, experience or goals, and it changes nothing in testing, FAR, Regression, seals, releases, Genesis or BRAIN.
