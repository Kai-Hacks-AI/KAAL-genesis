Making a Run, obtaining executions on the environments a Plan requires, and combining the Runs are three responsibilities, and none is a Skill's or an Extension's of KAAL's composition. The Environment Extension identifies the environment of the host it is already executing on and makes the Plan's Run there: it translates that host to its environment and performs the instances under it, and it obtains no execution anywhere else. Obtaining executions on the hosts, one per environment the Plan requires, belongs to the boundary that provides those hosts. For GitHub, hosted runners, jobs and the artifacts that carry a report between them are GitHub's facts and live in its workflows. What the Runs show together is Testing's evidence. KAAL's side is stated in its own terms: the Plan it derives, the Run each environment makes of it, and Testing's evidence over the reports. A required environment no job runs on is a Run nobody made, and Testing's evidence says the Plan is not evidenced; nothing supplies the missing Run. Until KAAL derives which Requirements are protected for a change, a workflow names them, as the Regression Test Plan is named, and that is a bootstrap and not a decision of GitHub's.
