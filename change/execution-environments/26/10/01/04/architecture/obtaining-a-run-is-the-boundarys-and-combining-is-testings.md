Obtaining the Run of a Plan from each environment it requires is work of the boundary that realizes those environments, and what the Runs show together is Testing's evidence: no Skill, and no Extension of KAAL's composition, holds either. For GitHub, hosted runners, jobs and the artifacts that carry a report between them are GitHub's facts and live in its workflows. KAAL's side is stated in its own terms: the Plan it derives, the Run each environment makes of it through the Environment Extension, and Testing's evidence over the reports. A required environment no job runs on is a Run nobody made, and Testing's evidence says the Plan is not evidenced; nothing supplies the missing Run. Until KAAL derives which Requirements are protected for a change, a workflow names them, as the Regression Test Plan is named, and that is a bootstrap and not a decision of GitHub's.
