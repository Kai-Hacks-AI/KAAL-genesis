---

Testing may require evidence under an opaque parameter such as environment = X, and it knows nothing of how X is provided; an environment adapter realizes X.

An agent needs the relevant environment for more than verification: to reproduce, inspect, edit, implement, debug, review and execute, as well as to produce Testing evidence. The boundary is therefore a request for an external realization, a workspace, carrier or service, and is not test orchestration.
