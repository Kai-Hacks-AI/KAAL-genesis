id: core-is-the-foundation-skills-and-extensions-depend-on
---

Core is the shared foundation of an installed KAAL: machinery that Skills and Extensions may depend on, and that depends on neither.

Core depends on no Skill and no Extension, so what it holds can be used by both without either being a precondition of it. It holds only machinery that more than one Skill or Extension needs and that no Skill owns as its own semantics: it holds no Skill's model, no composition of Skills and no fact of an external system. The pressure is real in today's code: six Skills each carry the same portable-name rule, and three carry identical id and front-matter handling, with nothing shared to hold it. A Skill depending on Core is therefore expected and is no violation of its independence. No installed Core exists yet, and nothing in the repository is Core today, which includes Genesis. Importance never makes machinery Core: sealing, Requirements and Testing are all needed by KAAL, and each is a Skill or an Extension. Core is not a home for what matters.
