id: core-births-kaal-and-operates-before-it-exists
---

Core is the minimum machinery that births KAAL, and it can operate before KAAL exists.

Core composes Skills to bring a KAAL into being from an empty place. By what it composes Core is indistinguishable from an Extension; what distinguishes it is that Core can operate before KAAL exists, while an Extension presupposes a KAAL to extend. Operating before KAAL exists, Core cannot depend on an Extension, and the Skills it composes are what it has. Whether Core also has a part once KAAL is installed, such as discovering or loading what is installed, is not decided. That nothing imports Genesis today is an observation about today's code, not a rule. Importance never makes machinery Core: sealing, Requirements and Testing are all needed by KAAL, and each is a Skill or an Extension. Core is not a home for what matters.
