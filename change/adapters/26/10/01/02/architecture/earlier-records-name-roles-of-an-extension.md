Earlier records name roles of an Extension and KAAL's own side of a boundary, and the Agent is not a component.

Records and material written before this vocabulary are immutable, and use KAAL core, or core, for KAAL's own meaning on its side of a boundary, adapter for an Extension in its boundary role, and composition for an Extension in its composing role. Core, capitalized, now names only the shared foundation that Skills and Extensions depend on; the operation that births KAAL is bootstrapping, and is not Core. Core, Skill and Extension classify machinery. BRAIN, Changes, Requirements, Defects, Architecture records and AGENTS.md are KAAL's material: born or placed by machinery and never machinery themselves, whichever folder holds them. The Agent uses KAAL and is none of them; AGENTS.md is the entry point it is given.
