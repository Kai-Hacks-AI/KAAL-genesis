---
id: required-instances-are-an-extension
---

The composition that derives the instances a Plan requires from Requirements is an Extension.

Managing Requirements and Testing are each a Skill and know nothing of each other. The decision that a Requirement's HOW is required under parameters, and the derivation of a Plan's instances from it, relate them, so they belong to neither Skill: they are an Extension in its composing role, kept in KAAL's Test composition with its Test Cases and Defects. The provider-neutral environment parameter it names is KAAL meaning stated there. How a host is observed to be linux or windows is the boundary role of another Extension, which this one neither contains nor depends on.
