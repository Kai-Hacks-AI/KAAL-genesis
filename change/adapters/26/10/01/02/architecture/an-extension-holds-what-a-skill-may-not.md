---
id: an-extension-holds-what-a-skill-may-not
---

An Extension holds what no Skill may hold, and it is one concept in two roles.

Machinery that is specific to KAAL, or to an external system, and is not what births KAAL, is an Extension. In its composing role an Extension composes independent Skills into KAAL-specific behaviour: it states how Skills relate, what KAAL means by their combination and which decisions KAAL takes over them. In its boundary role it realizes a boundary between KAAL and an external system or environment: it states the need in KAAL's terms and owns every fact that is true only of that system, as an adapter does in the records of the adapters Change. One Extension, or one function of it, may play both roles, as a single file does today, so a role is judged by what the machinery does and never by its folder or its name. Both roles are one concept because one rule places them: whatever knows KAAL's combination of Skills, or an external system's facts, lives outside every Skill, so that every Skill stays closed to both. They share no interface and no lifecycle, and a boundary's contract is found from the operation that needs it. An Extension is not optional because it is an Extension: the name states placement and dependency, never whether KAAL can run without it.

An Extension depends on the Skills it composes, on other Extensions without a cycle, and on the external system it realizes. Nothing it depends on depends back on it: a Skill receives what an Extension produces as an argument from its caller, never by importing it.
