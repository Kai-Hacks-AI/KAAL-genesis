---
id: core-births-kaal-and-presupposes-nothing
---

Core is the minimum machinery that births KAAL, and it presupposes nothing of KAAL.

Core composes Skills to bring a KAAL into being from an empty place, so that Extensions then have a KAAL to extend. It depends on Skills only, never on an Extension, and nothing depends on Core: once KAAL exists, nothing needs Core to run again. By what it composes Core is indistinguishable from an Extension; it is Core because it is first, since no Extension and no earlier KAAL material is needed for it to run, and extending presupposes a KAAL to extend. Importance never makes machinery Core: sealing, Requirements and Testing are all needed by KAAL, and each is a Skill or an Extension. Core is not a home for what matters.
