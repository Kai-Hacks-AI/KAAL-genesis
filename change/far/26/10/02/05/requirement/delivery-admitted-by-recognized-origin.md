---
id: delivery-admitted-by-recognized-origin
---

A delivery into an accepted line is admitted only from an origin that the line's owner recognizes; a delivery from any other origin is refused.

This states what admission requires. It holds whether KAAL engineers itself, is embedded in what is engineered, or looks at something it does not impose itself on; who owns a line, which origins they recognize and how an origin is established are for the realization of delivery to state, and no provider fact defines them.
