id: a-far-record-is-the-identity-of-the-hit-it-records
---

A FAR record is the identity of the Hit it records, and the Runs it admits state it.

FAR walks accepted states in order, one record per Hit. A record needs no name for its Hit taken from anywhere else: the record's own identity, its Change occurrence `far/YY/MM/DD/CC`, is the identity of the Hit it is about. That identity is opaque to Testing, which retains it with a Run and never reads it. Each Run FAR admits states it as its candidate identity. FAR judges that stated identity, which Testing does not: every admitted Run states the identity of the record that holds it. Testing judges what the Runs show of the Plan: together they evidence it. The Plan and each Run are kept in the record and sealed with it once, after they are made, because the state they tested is the Hit, which the record does not contain. Each Run says where it executed only as a placeholder, so the record keeps no location of its own.

A boundary realizes the Hit identity as the candidate workspace and demonstrates that realization; that mapping is the boundary's and is never read from the record. A record still says which Hit it is about, what it requires of it and what Runs showed it, wherever it is read.
