---
id: environment-in-check-name-only-where-asserted
---

The environment a check runs in appears in its name only when the environment is part of what it asserts.

This states what naming requires. It holds whether KAAL engineers itself, is embedded in what is engineered, or looks at something it does not impose itself on; how a check is defined, where it runs and where its name is shown are for the realization of delivery to state, and no provider fact defines them.
