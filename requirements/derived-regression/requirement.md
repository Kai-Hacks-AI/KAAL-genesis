---
holds: The next regression is derived from the accepted regression, what the
  candidate explicitly gives up and what it newly promises and demonstrates, by
  the same operation in every generation.
---

KAAL derives the regression a candidate would carry as the next accepted regression from three things alone, read from files: the accepted regression, what the candidate explicitly gives up of it in the acceptance records it adds, and what the candidate newly promises and demonstrates by its own cases. The next regression is what the accepted regression protects, as its plan requires it and its cases show it, less exactly what those records give up, with each demonstrated new promise and the cases that demonstrate it. Nothing the candidate states otherwise, such as its own Regression Plan, decides what survives or what enters: a candidate is accepted only when its own regression carries the derived one exactly.

Holding it means that the derivation is repeatable: the regression it derives, read from the candidate's files, is the accepted regression the same derivation takes as its input next, with no knowledge of how it came to be, no Git history and no record of a pull request. A candidate that gives up nothing and newly promises nothing carries the accepted protection unchanged.

It ends there. It does not decide whether giving something up is wise, or whether a candidate should be accepted at all; it does not give up or add conditions, proof other than cases or data, which carry over as they were; it does not accept, seal or promote the regression it derives; and it does not establish the first regression of a KAAL, which has no regression before it.
