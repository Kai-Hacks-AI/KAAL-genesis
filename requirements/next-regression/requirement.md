---
holds: The next regression is derived from the accepted regression, what the
  candidate explicitly gives up, what it newly promises and demonstrates, and
  how it evolves the protection definition without reducing it, by the same
  operation in every generation.
---

KAAL derives the regression a candidate would carry as the next accepted regression from the accepted regression and the candidate's files alone. What it protects is what the accepted regression protects, less exactly what the acceptance records the candidate adds give up, with each demonstrated new promise and the cases that demonstrate it. How it demonstrates that protection is the candidate's own regression, where every way it differs from that derivation is a change to the protection definition classified as preserved or strengthened, as `requirements/protection-evolution/requirement.md` states. A candidate with any change classified otherwise, or that leaves out or adds protected meaning, has no next regression, and is refused.

Holding it means that the derivation is repeatable: the regression it derives, read from the candidate's files, is the accepted regression the same derivation takes as its input next, with no knowledge of how it came to be, no Git history and no record of a pull request. A candidate that gives up nothing, newly promises nothing and changes no protection definition carries the accepted protection unchanged.

It ends there. It does not decide whether giving something up is wise, or whether a candidate should be accepted at all; it does not give up conditions or proof other than cases, which carry over unless more are added; it does not accept, seal or promote the regression it derives; and it does not establish the first regression of a KAAL, which has no regression before it.
