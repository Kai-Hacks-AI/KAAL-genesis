---
holds: The next regression is derived from the accepted regression, what the
  candidate explicitly gives up, and what it newly promises and demonstrates,
  and projects the accepted evidence that demonstrates it, by the same
  operation in every generation.
---

KAAL derives the regression a candidate would carry as the next accepted regression from the accepted regression and the candidate's files alone. What it protects is what the accepted regression protects, less exactly what the acceptance records the candidate adds give up, with each demonstrated new promise. Its plan requires exactly that: the same commitments, each shown as the accepted plan shows it, a new one by its cases, the same suites, conditions, proof and data. The evidence that demonstrates it is the accepted regression's own, projected as `requirements/protection-evolution/requirement.md` states, with what the candidate admits beside it. A candidate whose plan leaves out or adds protected meaning, or that does not hold exactly the evidence projected, has no next regression, and is refused.

Holding it means that the derivation is repeatable: the regression it derives, read from the candidate's files, is the accepted regression the same derivation takes as its input next, with no knowledge of how it came to be, no Git history and no record of a pull request. A candidate that gives up nothing and newly promises nothing carries the accepted protection and its evidence unchanged, whatever it does with its own testing.

It ends there. It does not decide whether giving something up is wise, or whether a candidate should be accepted at all; it does not give up conditions or proof other than cases, which carry over unless more are added; it does not accept, seal or promote the regression it derives; and it does not establish the first regression of a KAAL, which has no regression before it.
