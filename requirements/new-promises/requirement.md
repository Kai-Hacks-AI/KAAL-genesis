---
holds: KAAL names every commitment a candidate states that the accepted state
  does not, from both states' files alone, and nothing else
---

Given two states of KAAL's files, an accepted state and a candidate proposed to succeed it, KAAL names what the candidate newly promises: every commitment the candidate states that the accepted state does not. A state states a commitment by naming its place in its Regression Plan, or by recording a Requirement, whether or not a plan names it yet; a commitment is identified by that place, as a case points at it. KAAL reads only the two states' files, as directories, and asks neither Git nor GitHub which state is which.

What the candidate newly promises depends only on what the two states state. It does not change with the candidate's code, cases, suites, test data or runs, nor with how they are arranged, so a candidate that states nothing new newly promises nothing.

It names nothing else. What the accepted state states and the candidate does not is never named, neither as a new promise nor as anything given up: the candidate's omission is read as nothing at all. A Requirement the candidate holds at a place the accepted state also records it, but with a different record, is refused rather than read as kept or as newly promised.

Where this ends: whether the candidate may stop stating something the accepted state states, and what the accepted state's commitments must keep being shown by, are not part of this.
