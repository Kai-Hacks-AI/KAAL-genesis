# Managing Ideas

KAAL uses the **managing-ideas** skill to retain a possibility worth remembering without turning it into current meaning or committed work.

An Idea is not something KAAL currently believes. It is not roadmap commitment. BRAIN holds KAAL's learned and current meaning; a Defect records an observed failure worth retaining; an Idea records a possibility worth retaining without commitment. These durable records do not collapse into one another.

KAAL keeps its Ideas in `ideas/`. Each Idea records the possibility and enough context to understand why it was worth retaining. Later acceptance, rejection, implementation or better understanding does not rewrite the historical possibility, so an Idea is immutable. KAAL requires this of every Idea and does not yet seal Ideas with using-seals; until it does, an Idea's record is kept by never being written again.

If something later acts on or interprets an Idea, that later thing should generally point toward the immutable historical record. KAAL does not yet know the exact form of such a relationship, so none is built here.

The skill explains how Ideas are recorded and checked. This node records why and how KAAL uses it; the Ideas themselves are not BRAIN meaning.
