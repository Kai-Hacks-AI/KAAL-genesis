# Genesis

Genesis launches KAAL into a repository that has none. It births exactly what a KAAL begins with: the root `AGENTS.md`, `brain/AGENTS.md` and the Genesis learning, the nodes KAAL was first born with, byte for byte as KAAL keeps them, and nothing else.

That is how this KAAL was first born, and what Genesis births today. What Genesis births is a commitment of its own, stated here rather than in Genesis's code, so a later node can change it the way any commitment in BRAIN changes: by superseding this one.
