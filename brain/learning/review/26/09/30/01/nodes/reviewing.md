# Reviewing

KAAL uses the **reviewing** skill so that whether another repair and review round is justified is decided from the findings, the responsibility under review and what earlier rounds established, rather than by the Human stopping the loop from outside or by a reviewer continuing because another finding exists.

KAAL's work runs a loop of implementation, adversarial review, finding and repair. The loop finds real defects, but it did not know when another round was still useful, and that was being decided Bare, round by round. Its worked history is the evidence for moving the decision forward in BASS, from Bare to Skill. In #74 three findings each falsified what the work claimed, that a failed birth removes only what it created, never another Change, and never writes outside its root, and each had a repair that closed its class inside that responsibility; the third was introduced by the repair of the second and still justified a round. The work then stated the limit a path-based filesystem imposes, and the next review was clean. In #73 a Windows failure was real and belonged to the skills check, while the work's evidence could still be obtained, so it was disposed of and not absorbed. In #62 repairs that claimed a class closed were falsified by further members of it, four times for how a module is named; repairs that consulted what decides the class settled what they covered, until the class a disposition claimed became every input that decides how Node, tsx and esbuild interpret a case, something no one authority the work could consult decides. A finding then directly falsified the disposition that a missing configuration makes the settings unknown, while another showed the inputs incomplete. The owner stopped the loop there and the work was redesigned; From the findings shown by then, Reviewing reaches reconsider no later than the owner did, and without the owner. An imported helper's weakening was valid but could only be closed by Run, and the responsibility was restated with that gap: reconsider, then review resumed against the new statement. #75 was closed for its architectural context, not for anything review found, which is a decision about the responsibility from outside Reviewing.

That history is explained by one rule: a round is justified only while it can materially increase confidence that the work holds its responsibility without changing that responsibility. It was not explained by the number of rounds, by severity labels, or by whether a finding arose from an earlier repair, so Reviewing uses none of them. Semantic size is an observation, not part of the rule: as #62 grew, findings increasingly arose where its responsibility met ones owned by tools and the environment, and that shows up as falsifying findings with no bounded repair rather than as any measure of size.

Reviewing is a skill of instructions and no script: whether a finding falsifies what the work claims is judgement that is not yet understood well enough to be made deterministic, and a script that decided it would make its mistakes deterministic too. Its outcomes are few: continue, ready, blocked and reconsider.

Reviewing is independent: it reviews any responsibility, not a Change, and depends on no other skill, on testing, BRAIN, Git or a repository host, or on any reviewer. KAAL keeps no record of review: a finding and its reasoning stay with the work reviewed, a failure worth keeping becomes a defect, and a disposition is kept where the work keeps the reasoning behind it, stated so a later finding could falsify it.

The skill explains how to review. This node records why KAAL uses it.
