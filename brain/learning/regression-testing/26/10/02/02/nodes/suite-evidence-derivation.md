name: suite-evidence-derivation
---

KAAL understands, later than the Testing learning born with #172, that a Suite's claim is evidenced only if the Plan derived for what it names holds the Suite's evidence. FAR-15's first composite Suite showed that a Plan derived from Test Cases alone held nothing for a Requirement only a Suite claims, so it demonstrated nothing of it. Testing therefore computes, from the Suites given, the Case files beneath each Suite whose `tests` names a target, and KAAL's composition finds the Suites beneath each Change's `test/`, holds their `tests` to the kinds and ids a Test Case's are held to, and adds those Cases to the Plans it derives. This is scoped to today's representation of a Suite, where its Cases are the Case files beneath its directory. It is not the general definition of how a Suite and its Cases compose, and it settles nothing about a Case that several Suites would share, since two Carrier paths are two Carriers. The Testing learning of #172 stays as it was learned; this refers back to it and does not rewrite it.
