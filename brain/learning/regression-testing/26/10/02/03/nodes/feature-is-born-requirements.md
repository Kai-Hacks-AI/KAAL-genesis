name: feature-is-born-requirements
---

KAAL understands that the Feature of a Change is the Requirements born on it, a deterministic projection and not a separate handwritten judgment. FAR-15 showed why: once its Requirement was honestly decomposed into two, one of them could have vanished from a handwritten Feature only because Testing could not yet defend it, which would have let discovery be edited to what can currently be protected. Testing already owns the Regression computation, so it owns the smallest Feature projection too, computeFeature, and KAAL composes it with the Requirements a Change keeps in its own requirement directory. Regression is then old Regression, less Authorise, plus that Feature. This is born in Testing for now, and where it belongs may move if repeated use shows a better home. It does not change Authorise, Requirement management or FAR, and Authorise remains handwritten.
